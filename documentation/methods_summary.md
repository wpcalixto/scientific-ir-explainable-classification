# Methods summary

Three domains (microarrays, metamaterials, radiomics) and three search strategies (A: with HST; B: broad, without HST; C: proposed protocol)
were executed in OpenAlex and replicated on Scopus, Web of Science, Semantic Scholar and Elicit on 2026-11-10. The canonical corpus was
labelled (Applied / Methodological / General) by an LLM and used to train and explain four classifiers (SHAP, LIME, ablation, public benchmarks).
Retrieval quality was measured with a panel of three LLM judges, a human top-10 census and a stratified human validation of P@100,
with an independent replication. Details: article *Protocol for scientific information retrieval with explainable text classification: design and cross-domain evaluation*.
