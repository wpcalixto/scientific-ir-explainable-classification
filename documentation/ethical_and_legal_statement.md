# Ethical and legal statement

The study uses bibliographic metadata returned by public and institutional search services (OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, Scopus,
Web of Science, Elicit) and public screening benchmarks. No personal data of the authors of the indexed publications beyond the
bibliographic metadata published by these services is processed.
Relevance judgements were given by volunteer assessors on bibliographic records only; no data about the assessors themselves were
analysed. Assessors are identified only by letters (K–W, X–Z) and team codes; names, institutions and the original sheets are not included.
Users must comply with the terms of each source platform and cite the original benchmarks.
